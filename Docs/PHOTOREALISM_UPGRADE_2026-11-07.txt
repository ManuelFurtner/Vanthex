TEXTURE AND MATERIAL AUDIT
Source images and texture metadata were inspected, not selected by filename alone.
Several 4096-square inputs were model-specific atlases and were rejected for
arbitrary world tiling. Reused existing local Fab/project PBR sets:
 /Game/Textures/T_FrontConcrete01_{BC,N,ORM} (2048-square source)
 /Game/Textures/T_FactoryConcreteFloor01_{BC,N,ORM} (2048-square source)
These source textures were referenced directly, not copied or edited.
Cast concrete and floors use physical 400 cm projection tiles with signed surface
bases; the projection keeps normal orientation consistent on opposing wall faces.
Correct source colour-space and normal/mask sampling are used. Two small opaque
master variants support existing linear-color versus mask-compressed ORM inputs.

New materials are separate MI_P_* instances in /Game/Vanthex/Blender/Photoreal.
The initial Refinery material set remains available in the fallback backup.
Changes include fuller texture-backed roughness variation, concrete formwork and
subtle imperfections, coated-steel edge highlights, brushed aluminium/copper,
dielectric rubber/plastic and a separate translucent instrument-glass material.
ArtSource/Photoreal/Textures contains three original 2048-square zinc maps. Crystal
scale is 6.25 mm in a 100 cm tile. These are authored finish maps, not scanned data.
Existing original brushed-metal/rubber/paint textures are reused where suitable.
No blanket procedural grunge or new texture atlas is applied to every surface.

LIGHTING AUDIT AND FINAL SETTINGS
Runtime audit confirms Lumen GI=1, Lumen reflections=1, virtual shadow maps=1 and
both screen-trace paths enabled. Extended EV100 range is disabled, so the existing
0.32-0.50 exposure bounds are luminance values, not EV100. Exposure bias remains -1.5.
The pass does not silently change project-wide exposure conventions.
Final Gather quality 1.25; Scene Lighting quality 1.0; Reflection quality 1.25.
Bloom reduced to 0.07; existing restrained fog remains. No added cinematic fog.
Photoreal rect key intensities use the existing lumen input times 0.18; point fills
use times 0.07. Key contact-shadow length is 0.025 and bias is 0.35. Fixtures remain
attached to traced ceiling surfaces. The final inventory is in FullLightingAudit.txt.
No new dynamic light population, path tracing or offline render substitutes were used.
Reflection captures were not added indiscriminately to this Lumen-lit interior.

CORRECTION PASSES AND MEASURED DEFECTS
1. Initial material preview exposed an ORM sampler mismatch; fixed before acceptance.
2. Metal highlights exposed faceted shading. Curved normals were repaired, roughness
   was moderated and unstable anisotropy was disabled. No anisotropic BRDF claim is
   made for the final material; brushed texture detail remains.
3. Unused Glass palette slots caused opaque Nanite meshes to fall back to raster
   proxies. Initial draw calls rose above 1,300. Import now removes empty material
   groups. Only the small glass-covered monitor uses ordinary raster geometry.
4. Core illumination and Lumen quality were corrected to preserve surface texture,
   contact and competitive readability while controlling cost.
5. Back-facing and unsupported visual electronics were repositioned/oriented.

similar room silhouettes and some sparse areas still reveal procedural assembly.

is needed across displays and quality settings. Machinery is largely static; the
new indicator is cosmetic. Characters and weapons retain their prior visual quality.

freedom from visual intersections is made. No purchase, paid service, Edgegap
deployment, GitHub upload or publication of raw licensed maps was performed.
