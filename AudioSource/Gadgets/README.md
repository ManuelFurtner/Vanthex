# Vanthex gadget presentation sources

Fifteen original deterministic mono WAVs support Flash, Smoke, Frag, EMP and Sensor presentation. No downloaded recordings or third-party samples are used. Regenerate with `python AudioSource/Gadgets/generate_gadget_audio.py`; the standard-library generator writes 48 kHz PCM16 with approximately 6.38 dB peak headroom and a SHA-256 manifest.

After building, import with `UnrealEditor-Cmd.exe Vanthex.uproject -run=VanthexBuildGadgetPresentation -unattended -nop4`. Use `-ReplacePrototypeAssets` to refresh generated assets. The commandlet creates `/Game/Vanthex/Gadgets/DA_GadgetPresentation`, 15 audio assets, opaque smoke and emissive materials, two spatial attenuation settings and a shared 20-voice concurrency limit. Cook `/Game/Vanthex/Gadgets`.

`UVanthexGadgetVisualComponent` is cosmetic. The replicated gameplay actor calls `Configure(GadgetId)`, `ActivateEffect(Duration, Radius, ElapsedSeconds)`, `EndEffect()`, `PlayBounce()` and `PulseEffect()`. Sensor pulses follow real server scans; presentation has no independent scan or damage logic. Late activation skips expired one-shot sounds. Flight, smoke hiss and EMP hum stop at activation/end/destruction as applicable; bounce sounds have a 120 ms minimum interval. Near sounds fall off within 18 m; activation blasts use 65 m maximum range and physical occlusion filtering.

Smoke uses overlapping opaque, two-sided sphere volumes with animated world-space surface noise, centered at the grenade's world position plus 140 cm in Z. Candidate lobes are visibility-tested from that center, then bounded by six local obstacle traces; unreachable or tiny lobes are omitted. This is dense geometric smoke, not physically simulated volumetric fluid. Gameplay owns its separate smoke/LOS rules. Inside/outside appearance and alignment with gameplay need rendered inspection. Flash/Frag have a brief light burst and sparks; EMP and actual Sensor pulses have expanding emissive rings. Throwable silhouettes and color details distinguish all five types.

Automation groups `Vanthex.Gadgets.PresentationAssets` and `Vanthex.Gadgets.SmokeWallClipping` check asset completeness, mono/loop configuration, opaque two-sided smoke, world-up center, lifecycle and wall clipping. Passing builds, automation, rendering and mixer results must be recorded after execution; no such result is assumed by this source document.
